# Micro-SaaS Market Research 2026 (compiled from live web research)

## Market context

- Global SaaS market: ~$399B (2024), projected $819B by 2030
- Micro-SaaS segment growing ~30% annually: $15.7B (2024) -> $59.6B projected (2030)
- Typical micro-SaaS: 1-5 people, $50K-$3M+/yr revenue, profitable in 1-2 years
- Build-to-launch window: 4-12 weeks for focused MVPs

## Validated idea spaces (with proven demand)

1. AI content repurposing - turn one podcast/video into LinkedIn posts, newsletters, short scripts. Gap: tools distribute but do not transform. $349/yr+ pricing precedent.
2. Niche job boards - employers pay $250+/posting on vertical boards; 67% of qualified candidates buried by generic platform algorithms.
3. Client portals for service businesses - freelance management market ~$4.2B (2025); price $29-49/mo for solo consultants.
4. Subscription payment recovery - SaaS loses ~9% MRR to failed payments; tool recovering 20-30% sells itself at $50-100/mo.
5. Vertical AI meeting notes - $3.24B market (2025); win by targeting one sector (legal, healthcare) instead of horizontal competition.
6. Micro-influencer outreach manager - discovery + contracts + deliverable tracking for small brands priced out of enterprise tools.
7. Freelancer proposal generator - Q&A wizard -> branded PDF + payment link. $10-40/mo, clear time savings.
8. Testimonial collector - embeddable widgets from a shareable form; Famewall hit $1K MRR in 12 months doing exactly this.
9. Appointment scheduling for niche services - vertical-specific booking (not generic calendars).
10. E-commerce return/exchange automator - higher complexity but real pain.

## What winning micro-SaaS ideas share

- Solve one specific problem for one defined audience
- Audience already pays for worse solutions
- Recurring-revenue model at $10-100/mo
- Buildable MVP in weeks, not months

## Proof points

- Plann (social planner): non-technical founder, $1M revenue in 2 years
- Subscribr: pre-sold 50 lifetime deals (~$20K) before writing code
