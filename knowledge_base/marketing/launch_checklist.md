# Launch Checklist - Growth Playbook

## Before launch

- One-line positioning: who it is for + what pain it removes
- Landing page: headline, 3 benefits, screenshot, single CTA
- Analytics on signup funnel: visit -> signup -> activation
- Launch post drafted for Product Hunt / Hacker News / relevant communities

## Launch day

- Post to 2-3 channels maximum; do not spray
- Respond to every comment within an hour
- Capture emails even from visitors who do not sign up

## After launch

- Follow up every signup with a personal-style email within 24h
- Ask early users one question: "What almost stopped you from signing up?"
- Publish one piece of content per week answering a real user question
- Track which channel produced activated users, not just clicks

## Messaging rules

- Lead with the user's outcome, never the technology
- Show the product working in the first screenshot
- Specific numbers beat adjectives ("deploys in 30s" beats "fast")
